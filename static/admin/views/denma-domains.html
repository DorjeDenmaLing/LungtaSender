Gmail Postmaster Tools

Gmail's own complaint rate for each domain, by day: Gmail users who reported its mail as spam, as a share of its mail in their inboxes. Gmail asks senders to stay under 0.1% and never reach 0.3%. Centers see their own domain's on their dashboard. The hub reads them from a Google account that has the domains verified in Postmaster Tools.

Google client
Setting it up
  1. In Google Cloud, enable the Gmail Postmaster Tools API.
  2. Set up the OAuth consent screen as Internal (or publish it: in Testing, the access ends after 7 days).
  3. Make an OAuth client of the type Web application, with this authorized redirect URI:
  4. Save its ID and secret here, then connect with a Google account that has each domain verified in Postmaster Tools.

The hub asks only to read Postmaster Tools' figures, and fetches them every 6 hours. Gmail gives them only for days with enough mail to Gmail users.
