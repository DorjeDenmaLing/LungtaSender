Webhooks

Shared Server webhooks

For a system that signs people up at many centers and vouches for their consent, such as the Shambhala Database. It has one secret and posts to each center's /c/<center>/signup/server. A center's admins turn it on, choosing its lists, on Config → Webhooks; until they do, its requests there are refused. Each center's own limits apply.
